Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Short version: your photos are read on your phone and stay on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Camera and photos

Plyscribe asks for the camera so you can photograph a scoresheet with Apple's document scanner. If you prefer, you pick photos from your library instead; the app sees only the photos you choose.

The text on the sheet is recognised by Apple's Vision framework, on the device. Nothing is sent to us or to any server for reading. Plyscribe does not run a server of its own.

What is stored, and where

For every game you scan, the app saves on your device the page photo, what was read from each cell, your corrections, and the names, event and result you type. This lives inside the app's storage and is removed when you delete the game or the app. If you export a PGN, it goes where you send it: the share sheet, the clipboard or a file you choose.

Only when you tap "Open on Lichess" is that game's text (its moves and headers) sent to lichess.org, under Lichess's own privacy policy. Nothing else leaves the device.

Purchases

Plyscribe Pro is a one-time purchase made through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and we never see your card. To keep sales figures and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user identifier, the purchase record (product, date, store country) and technical device information. It does not receive your photos, games or any information about what you scanned. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No advertising and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No analytics about what is on your sheets or how you play.
  • No sale or sharing of data.

Children

Plyscribe is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices

Delete a game by swiping it in the list, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. You can turn off camera and photo access at any time in iOS Settings. To ask about purchase data held by RevenueCat, write to the address below.

Changes and contact
