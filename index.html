Plyscribe

Paper in, PGN out.

Plyscribe is an iPhone and iPad app for tournament players. Photograph the handwritten scoresheet and get the game back as a clean PGN.

How it reads

The app finds the grid of the sheet, reads the cells, and then replays the game from the first move. At every half-move it keeps only the moves the rules allow and picks the one that best matches the handwriting. A smudge cannot become an impossible move, and one bad cell does not ruin the rest.

It works best with neat print or block letters; cursive is read poorly and needs more taps.

Cells it is unsure of are marked in red. Tap one to see your own handwriting next to the legal moves and choose the right one.

Export

Share the PGN, copy it, or save it to Files, with event, round, players and result filled in. Plyscribe Pro adds Elo, time control, ply count and event date tags.
